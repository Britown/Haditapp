## Brand & Style

This design system delivers financial reconciliation with an effortless, calming presence. Combining the quiet luxury and rigorous craftsmanship of Apple hardware interfaces with the warm, human hospitality of Airbnb, the experience transforms co-parenting and shared household finances from friction-heavy chore into an act of collaborative clarity.

### Personality & Core Pillars
- **Serenity Over Stress:** Interface surfaces breathe. Financial ledgers avoid claustrophobic grids in favor of generous margins and tactile softness.
- **Radical Neutrality & Fairness:** Shared expense allocation between households requires balanced visual weight. Accents provide distinct identity without visual bias or hierarchy.
- **Tactile Minimalism:** High-definition micro-interactions, silky ambient shadows, and rich pitch-black actions create an atmosphere of precision craftsmanship.

### Target Audience
Modern co-parents, dual-household guardians, and collaborative partners seeking clear financial balance sheets, child-related expense settlement, and savings milestones without emotional friction or bureaucratic complexity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined canvas built on Apple-grade light tones with distinct functional roles:

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#F5F5F7`):** Warm, calming off-white substrate that relieves eye strain and eliminates digital glare.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure optical white creating pristine floating planes against the base canvas.
- **Subtle Borders (`#E5E5EA`):** Hairline structure that holds shapes with whisper-soft contrast (1px solid).
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#EFEFF4`):** Recessed containers, input fills, and segmented control wells.
- **Primary Typography (`#1D1D1F`):** Rich charcoal-black delivering maximum legibility without harsh absolute black contrast.
- **Secondary Typography (`#6E6E73`):** Neutral gray for supporting metadata, balance labels, and breadcrumbs.
- **Muted Typography (`#86868B`):** Tertiary timestamps and disabled states.

### Meaningful Accents
- **Action Black (`#000000`):** Pitch black reserved for high-impact interactions, primary call-to-actions, and confirmed settlement moments.
- **Father Share / Paternal Allocation (`#2C6BED`):** Soft modern azure blue for clear visual lineage in splits and balances.
- **Mother Share / Maternal Allocation (`#8B5CF6`):** Delicate, refined iris lilac balancing the paternal blue with equal visual luminesce.
- **Benefits & Positive Delta (`#10B981`):** Calibrated emerald green indicating settled transactions, escrow confirmations, and accrued savings.
- **Alert & Dispute (`#FF3B30`):** Muted crimson reserved exclusively for action-required balance discrepancies or failed synchronizations.

## Typography

Typography relies on geometric precision, proportional figures, and tight letter-spacing mimicking native Apple ecosystem typography. 

### Principles
- **Tabular Numerics:** All currency displays, balance figures, and tabular receipts must apply OpenType `tnum` (tabular numbers) to avoid shifting alignment during real-time reconciliations.
- **Negative Tracking:** Headings from 24px and above leverage slight negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) for a confident, editorial fintech presence.
- **Optical Hierarchy:** Critical figures use prominent semi-bold styling (`fontWeight: 600`), while descriptions and metadata remain light and airy in regular weights (`fontWeight: 400`) to safeguard spatial breathability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an airy, fluid-constrained grid that preserves focus and eliminates peripheral distractions.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment at `1160px` centered on canvas, structured using an 8-column layout for dashboards and a focused 6-column layout for reconciliation flows. Outer margins scale to `margin: 3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column fluid structure with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with edge-to-edge floating cards and bottom-anchored action zones. Outer margins compress to `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`.

### Spacing Cadence
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-xl` (40px) on desktop and transitions smoothly to `space-lg` (24px) on mobile viewports.
- Related balance breakdowns maintain an 8px (`space-sm`) rhythm; distinct conceptual sections (such as Pending Approvals vs. Settled Histories) utilize `space-xl` (40px) separations to maintain an unhurried, serene workflow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons hard drop shadows and heavy multi-layered borders. Depth is achieved via atmospheric diffusion and physical surface layering.

### The Ambient Layering Model
1. **Base Plate (`#F5F5F7`):** Flat foundational substrate.
2. **Resting Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Elevated using a dual-phase ambient shadow paired with a hairline border:
   - Primary Ambient Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`
   - Structural Border: `1px solid #E5E5EA`
3. **Hover & Interactive Card State:**
   - Elevated Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
   - Transform: Subtle `-2px` Y-axis lift over `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
4. **Modals & Elevated Sheets:**
   - Diffusion Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)` over `rgba(245, 245, 247, 0.72)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, friendly pill-dominant geometry that feels modern, approachable, and comforting:

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized on large radii between `24px` and `28px` (`rounded-xl`), evoking polished handheld devices.
- **Buttons & Chips:** Pure pill radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) delivering high touchability and friendly reassurance.
- **Form Inputs & Inner Segment Blocks:** `14px` to `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), creating nested concentric harmony inside cards.
- **Avatars & Balance Share Badges:** Circular (`9999px`) with 2px offset border rings separating profile artwork from card backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pitch Black Pill):** Background `#000000`, label `#FFFFFF`, pure pill curvature (`rounded-full`), height `48px` (desktop) / `52px` (mobile). Hover state: Background scales to `#1D1D1F` with a soft opacity transition (`transform: scale(0.99)` on active press).
- **Secondary Action:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E5E5EA`, black text `#1D1D1F`. Hover state: Background switches to `#F5F5F7`.
- **Tertiary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, neutral `#6E6E73` text, interactive hover tint `#EFEFF4`.

### Cards (Reconciliation Modules)
- Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `24px` radius on mobile, `28px` radius on desktop.
- Subtle hairline border (`1px solid #E5E5EA`).
- Internal layout must provide generous breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl` padding).
- Dynamic expense overview cards feature split balance bars displaying proportional shares (Azure for Papá, Lilac for Mamá).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill geometry (`rounded-full`), `32px` height, text in `label-sm` uppercase or `label-md`.
- **Paternal Allocation Tag:** Background `rgba(44, 107, 237, 0.08)`, text `#2C6BED`.
- **Maternal Allocation Tag:** Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`, text `#8B5CF6`.
- **Settled / Savings Badge:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)`, text `#059669`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Background `#F5F5F7` with invisible borders by default, transitioning to a crisp `#000000` 1.5px ring on focus.
- Height `52px` with `16px` inner border radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Floating or clear label in `label-sm` placed above inputs, paired with large legible placeholder text in `#86868B`.

### Lists & Transaction Rows
- Borderless rows separated by `1px solid #F5F5F7` dividing lines or segregated into distinct floating mini-capsules.
- Icon or household indicator in rounded squircle (`12px` radius) followed by transaction title, timestamp, and right-aligned tabular currency value.
- Clear split indicators showing each parent's proportional percentage directly below the amount.

### Split Reconciliation Slider (Specialized Component)
- Custom track with an integrated two-way draggable thumb.
- Left track filled with Azure (`#2C6BED`), right track filled with Lilac (`#8B5CF6`).
- Central pill displays exact ratio (e.g., `50% / 50%` or `60% / 40%`) with haptic-like snappy transition points.
